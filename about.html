About

A venture built on
what East Africa lacks.

Reliable software doesn't need a data center and a monthly invoice. It needs to survive the environment it runs in — and be owned by the people who use it.

Our position

Offline-first is not a fallback. It's the design.

Most software is built for stable power and unlimited bandwidth, then patched to survive Uganda. We build the other way around. Every product starts from the question: does this still work when the network drops, when the lights flicker, when the vendor's server is gone? If not, it isn't done.

◈

Data ownership is a product feature

Your business records are your capital. We design every system so the data physically lives on your machines, in an open format you can export at any time — never parked in a platform we control.
